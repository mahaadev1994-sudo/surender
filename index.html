Born in Nainital, Uttarakhand, amidst the serene Himalayas, Chef Surender brings a deep respect for organic seasonality, disciplined kitchen hierarchy, and passionate culinary artistry.

His journey bridges high-volume banquet excellence at JW Marriott Delhi with bespoke oceanfront luxury in the Maldives at Lavista by East Coast and Meat Street.
